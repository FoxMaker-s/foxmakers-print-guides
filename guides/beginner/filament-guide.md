# 🧵 Choosing Your First Filament — FoxMakers Guide

> PLA vs PETG vs ABS vs ASA vs TPU — which one should you use?

## TL;DR (Quick Decision)

```
Starting out?           → PLA
Need outdoor durability? → ASA
Need flexibility?        → TPU 95A
Need food-safe?          → PETG (uncolored)
Need high strength?      → PETG-CF or ABS
Have an enclosure?       → ABS / ASA / PA
```

## 🟢 PLA — Start Here

**Best for:** Prototypes, figurines, decorations, indoor parts  
**Not for:** High temperatures (>60°C), outdoor UV exposure

✅ Easiest to print — no warping, no enclosure, no drying needed  
✅ Available in every color and special variant (silk, matte, wood, glow)  
❌ Brittle under impact, degrades in UV and high heat

**Top brands:** Bambu Lab Basic, Prusament, Polymaker PolyLite

---

## 🔵 PETG — The All-Rounder

**Best for:** Mechanical parts, food containers, outdoor (short-term), clear parts  
**Not for:** Very high precision (slight shrinkage), very high temperatures

✅ Good layer adhesion, flexible, food-safe (uncolored + correct temp)  
✅ No enclosure needed  
❌ Strings more than PLA, sticks aggressively to some bed surfaces

**Settings:** 230-245°C nozzle, 80°C bed, 50% cooling fan

---

## 🔴 ABS — The Classic (but tricky)

**Best for:** High-temperature parts, automotive, enclosures  
**Not for:** Beginners without enclosure

✅ Heat resistant to 100°C+, machinable (sanding, drilling, acetone smoothing)  
❌ **Requires enclosed printer**, warps badly on open frame, fumes (ventilate!)

---

## 🟡 ASA — Better ABS

Same as ABS but **UV-resistant** — best for outdoor functional parts.  
Print settings identical to ABS. Worth the extra cost for outdoor applications.

---

## 🟣 TPU — Flexible Filament

**Best for:** Phone cases, gaskets, grips, wearables  
**Not for:** Bowden extruders (very difficult)

✅ Extremely durable, rubber-like, chemical resistant  
❌ Print slow (20-40mm/s), direct drive extruder strongly recommended

---
